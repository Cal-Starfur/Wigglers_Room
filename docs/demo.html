The game

What is Wigglers Room?

Your earthworm lives in a shared compost bin with other Reddit users. Eat food scraps, dig tunnel networks, deposit castings to enrich the soil, and manage the weekly tea drain — together.

No download. No app. Your Reddit account is your identity. The bin runs continuously whether you're there or not.

Controls — Mobile (Touch)

Touch gestures

👆
Single finger drag
Steers your worm toward your finger. The worm follows smoothly — drag slowly through food to eat it.
✌️
Two-finger tap — Poop
Deposits castings where your worm currently is. Depth matters — poop in the deep compost for maximum soil enrichment bonus.
⏱️
Long press 600ms — Sleep / Wake
Hold still for 0.6 seconds to toggle sleep mode. A glowing ring shows the timer filling. Must be in the compost layer (not the scraps zone) to sleep.
☝️
Swipe up — Lay cocoon
Flick upward quickly (60px+, under 400ms) to lay a cocoon. Earns karma. Costs nothing. Can only do this when alive and active.
🤟
Three-finger tap — Cycle avatar
Switches between your worm's three display modes. Cosmetic only.
🚰
Tap valve button — Drain control
When the tea reservoir is high or flooding, a valve button appears. Tap to open or close the emergency drain. Saving the bin earns Drain Hero karma.
📜
Drag (while sleeping) — Scroll bin
When your worm is sleeping, single-finger drag scrolls the camera so you can explore the full bin.

Advanced mechanics

Tunnels, drains & junctions

🌿 Tunnels

Your movement carves a tunnel network through the compost. Tea drops and poop follow your tunnel paths down to the sump. The better your network, the more efficiently the bin drains.

🕳️ Creating a drain point

Navigate to the sump (bottom layer) and hold completely still for ~1.5 seconds. A glowing ring appears. When it completes you create a down-drain — tea can now flow out. Earns +25 to +100 karma depending on whether you connected an existing tunnel segment.

🔀 Junctions

Hold still over an existing tunnel segment (not at the sump) for ~1.5 seconds to create a junction — connecting separate tunnel branches into a more efficient network. Earns karma and improves drainage for everyone sharing your tunnels.

💩 Strategic pooping

The deeper you poop in the compost zone, the more castingEnrichment you add to the shared soil. High enrichment means faster food spawns, quicker clog decay, and healthier tunnels — for every worm in the bin, not just you.

Community

The weekly tea cycle

☕ Tea reservoir

Everyone's poop deposits contribute to the tea reservoir all week. After 7 real days the cycle resets — top contributors earn karma bonuses. If the reservoir hits 90% before the reset, the bin floods and every active worm takes damage simultaneously.

🚨 Flood warning

Watch the tea level in the HUD. Above 80% you'll see a flood warning. Tap the valve button to trigger an emergency drain before it hits 90%. The player who saves the bin earns the Drain Hero bonus.

Life & death

When your worm dies

Causes of death

Acid poisoning (eating too much citrus or coffee without eggshell antidote) · Starvation (gut fully drained) · Flood damage (bin overflows while you're active) · Constipation (gut completely full, can't move)

🥚 The cocoon

When you die, a Reddit comment is automatically posted to the thread — your username, cause of death, how long you lived, your generation, and total karma. A cocoon appears at the death site. After 7 real days it hatches and you return as your own offspring with some karma carried forward.

🌱 Generations

Gen 1 is your first worm. Gen 2 is their child. Each generation starts a little ahead of the last — karma compounds across generations. Some players in the bin are already Gen 4 or Gen 5.

Quick tips

Things I wish I knew

  • Eggshell first, everything else second. Always. It's the most important item in the bin.
  • Poop deep. Surface deposits barely do anything. The compost layer depth bonus is huge.
  • Build drainage tunnels before you poop. Poop without a path just clogs the system.
  • Check the tea level before you log off. If it's above 75% and the weekly reset is days away, drain the valve.
  • Sleep in the compost zone, not the scraps layer. You get a warning if you try in the wrong zone.
  • Death is a chapter break, not a failure. Your Gen 2 worm starts with karma. Don't stress it.
  • Hold still over existing tunnels to create junctions — they earn karma and help the whole bin drain faster.
